Integrity Workstreams Dashboard

Automated Performance Analytics

A live, interactive dashboard that replaced 27.5 hours/month of manual compliance grading across 57 workstreams—refreshing daily from the source system.

⚠ The Problem

For Meta to meet its regulatory compliance obligations — the EU's Digital Services Act, Australia's Online Safety Act, and 10+ other jurisdictions — 57 cross-functional teams each had to regularly submit product updates, operational updates, and requirement completions into iWAM, the program's single source of truth. Without that information flowing in consistently, the program had no way to know whether Meta was actually on track. The grading system was the accountability mechanism: it assessed each team monthly across a weighted formula, made performance visible, and gave teams real incentive to stay current. When your grade goes to leadership, you make sure your updates are in.

The problem was that running that grading process manually took me 27.5 hours every month — pulling data from the regulatory requirements system, checking workflow completion, product updates, ops updates, and past-due requirements for all 57 tracks. The output was a static spreadsheet that was already stale by the time it was shared. Product and ops leads had no visibility into their own standing between cycles. Leadership couldn't get a current picture without asking me to stop everything and run the numbers. And the entire accountability system depended on one person doing 27.5 hours of manual work every month to make it function.

⚙ What I Built

I automated the entire grading process into a live, interactive dashboard that pulls directly from the source system and updates daily.

  • Overall grade for each of 57 workstreams — weighted composite score
  • Sub-grades broken out across 4 dimensions: workflow completion (50%), product updates (12.5%), ops updates (12.5%), past-due requirements (25%)
  • Drill-down tables showing which specific requirements are dragging scores down and why
  • Interactive filtering — click any track to see its full breakdown
  • Data sourced directly from the regulatory requirements system, refreshed daily
  • Every grade links back to the underlying requirements — full transparency into scoring

◎ Technical Integrations

  • Internal dashboarding platform — built natively within Meta's analytics infrastructure for production deployment
  • Regulatory requirements API — live data pulls from the source system, refreshing daily without manual intervention
  • Weighted scoring engine — codified the previously manual grading formula into automated calculations with full transparency into each component
  • Deep-link integration — every grade and sub-grade links directly to the underlying requirements for drill-down
  • Claude Code — used for development, iteration, and debugging throughout

✦ What I Learned

Real-time visibility changes behavior in ways that monthly reporting never could. Before this dashboard, teams found out they were behind at the monthly review — which meant they were always reacting, never preventing. Once they could check their grade any day, they started fixing issues before the review, not after. That shift — from reactive to proactive compliance management — was worth more than the 27.5 hours I reclaimed. The time savings were the headline; the behavior change was the actual impact.

Designing the grading formula was its own product problem. The weights — 50% workflow completion, 25% past-due requirements, 12.5% each for product and ops updates — weren't arbitrary. They were chosen to reflect actual execution health, not just activity. If product and ops updates had been weighted equally with workflow completion, teams could have inflated their grades without completing any real requirements. A measurement system that measures the wrong thing creates incentives to game the metric rather than improve the outcome.
