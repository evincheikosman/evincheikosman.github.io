Escalation Guide
How the escalation process works — from submission through decision and archival.
1
Submit a Topic
Use the 7-section submission form to document your escalation. Include context, risk level, stakeholders, and timeline constraints. The admin review queue is notified automatically on submission.
2
Reviewed & Accepted
The IRC team reviews your submission within 24 hours and assigns it to L1 SteerCo or L2 Circle based on scope and complexity. A shared GChat space is created automatically with all stakeholders added.
3
Preparation
A structured preparation deck is auto-generated and shared in your escalation space. Stakeholders review and contribute before the meeting. Scheduled reminders go out 48h and 24h prior.
4
Decision & Archive
After the meeting, the IRC team records the decision, rationale, and action items. The record is archived and indexed — queryable by topic, date, risk area, and stakeholder via the AI assistant.
